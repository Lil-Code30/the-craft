This section indexes external material worth keeping: where it lives, what it covers, and why it matters.

## The rules

- **Nothing is pre-populated.** Entries are added only when a resource is actually discovered, started, or recommended by a trusted source — never to make the list look full.
- **The index points, it does not copy.** Resources live at their origin; pages here record where to find them and why they earn a place.
- **Notes stay short.** A few lines per entry: what it covers, where it is, and its current status. Detailed notes belong in the relevant knowledge section, not here.
- **Traceability over lists.** A resource that produced real understanding is referenced from the pages it helped write, so every note in this documentation can be traced back to its sources.
