## The one-minute version

The Craft is a personal engineering practice with a simple loop:

1. Show up once a day and work on something small and technical.
2. Record what happened, lightly, in a session note.
3. When the result deserves a permanent place, distill it into this documentation.

The initial commitment is 90 days to rebuild the habit. The structure does not expire with it — sections, sessions, and tracking continue unchanged afterward.

## What this documentation is not

- It is not a course with a fixed order. Curiosity and practical need decide what comes next.
- It is not pre-populated. Empty areas mean "not explored yet", not "content missing".
- It is not a blog. Everything here is written to be useful later, not to be impressive now.
