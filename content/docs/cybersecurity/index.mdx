Security is studied here as a way of thinking: understanding how systems fail, who can make them fail, and how to reason about trust boundaries.

The practical core of this section is offense-informed defense — understanding attacks well enough to build systems that resist them.

## Areas

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| `fundamentals` | Core concepts — threat models, attack surfaces, authentication, authorization. |
| `web-security` | Vulnerabilities and defenses specific to web applications. |
| `cryptography` | Primitives, protocols, and — critically — how they are misused in practice. |
| `ctf` | Capture-the-flag exercises and write-ups. |

Pages appear in these areas as the subjects are actually explored. The folders are reserved, not pre-filled.
