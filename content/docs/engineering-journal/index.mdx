The journal is the thinking layer of The Craft. Daily sessions in the repository's `sessions/` directory record what happened on a given day; the journal keeps what deserves to survive the day.

The difference:

- A **session** is a raw log — quick to write, never revised.
- A **journal entry** is a distilled piece — written when something clicks, an experiment concludes, or a pattern becomes visible across several sessions.

## Areas

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| `reflections` | Thinking about the work itself — what is being learned about systems, and about how I learn. |
| `experiments` | Write-ups of experiments worth keeping: the hypothesis, the method, the result. |
| `reviews` | Periodic retrospectives — what went well, what stalled, what changes next. |

## Relationship to the rest of the repository

- Raw activity records live in `sessions/`, rendered read-only on this site under `/sessions`.
- The technical knowledge itself lives in the knowledge sections — the journal records the *process* of acquiring it.

Entries appear as the work produces them. The folders are reserved, not pre-filled.
