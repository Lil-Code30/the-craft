Prodigo is a long-term project developed in its own repository. This section is reserved for the architectural thinking around it — the kind of material that outlives individual commits and is worth reviewing years later.

## Areas

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| `architecture` | System structure — components, boundaries, data flow, and the reasons behind them. |
| `decisions` | Architecture Decision Records: context, options considered, the decision, and its consequences. |
| `research` | Technical investigations that inform design — comparisons, benchmarks, prior art. |
| `experiments` | Prototypes and design experiments that validate (or invalidate) a direction before it is built. |

## Scope

What belongs here:

- The reasoning behind structural choices.
- Decision records written while the decision is still fresh.
- Research and experiments that shaped the system's direction.

What does not belong here:

- Source code, commits, and task tracking — Prodigo's implementation lives in its own repository and continues independently.
- General software engineering knowledge — that belongs in [Software Engineering](/docs/software-engineering); only project-specific conclusions land here.

The Craft documents the thinking. The Prodigo repository does the building.
