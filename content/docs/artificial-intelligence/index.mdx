This section covers AI from first principles to applied engineering: what the models actually do, how they are built and trained, and how they are turned into working systems.

The emphasis is on understanding rather than hype — what a technique can and cannot do, and why.

## Areas

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| `fundamentals` | Math, search, knowledge representation, and the core ideas of the field. |
| `machine-learning` | Learning paradigms, model families, training, and evaluation. |
| `llms` | Large language models — architecture, capabilities, limits. |
| `ai-engineering` | Building with models — prompting, retrieval, evaluation pipelines, deployment. |
| `agents` | Autonomous and tool-using systems — design, reliability, and failure modes. |

Pages appear in these areas as the subjects are actually explored. The folders are reserved, not pre-filled.
