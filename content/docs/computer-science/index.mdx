This section holds the foundations: what computation is, how algorithms behave, how operating systems, databases, and networks work at their core.

The boundary with [Systems & Infrastructure](/docs/systems-and-infrastructure) is deliberate:

- **Computer Science** records the theory — concepts, models, and the reasoning behind them.
- **Systems & Infrastructure** records the practice — the same subjects as deployed, configured, and operated systems.

When a subject is explored from both angles, the pages cross-link instead of duplicating each other.

## Areas

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| `programming` | Languages, paradigms, type systems, and semantics. |
| `algorithms` | Design, analysis, correctness, and complexity. |
| `data-structures` | How data is organized, and why one structure fits a problem. |
| `operating-systems` | Processes, memory, scheduling, concurrency — the theory. |
| `databases` | Storage models, query processing, transactions, consistency. |
| `networking` | Protocols and models — how machines communicate. |

Pages appear in these areas as the subjects are actually explored. The folders are reserved, not pre-filled.
