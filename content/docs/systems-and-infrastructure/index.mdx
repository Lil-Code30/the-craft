This is where theory meets operations: the systems software actually runs on, and the infrastructure that keeps it running.

The boundary with [Computer Science](/docs/computer-science) is deliberate:

- **Computer Science** records the theory — concepts, models, and the reasoning behind them.
- **Systems & Infrastructure** records the practice — configuration, diagnostics, tooling, and operational lessons.

When a subject is explored from both angles, the pages cross-link instead of duplicating each other.

## Areas

| Area | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| `linux` | Daily driving — the shell, tooling, and the internals worth knowing. |
| `operating-systems` | OS concepts as they show up on real systems. |
| `docker` | Containers, images, and orchestration basics. |
| `networking` | Real networks — configuration, diagnostics, protocols in use. |
| `devops` | CI/CD, automation, observability, and incident practices. |
| `cloud` | Managed services, deployment models, and cost awareness. |
| `distributed-systems` | Coordination, replication, and consistency at scale. |

Pages appear in these areas as the subjects are actually explored. The folders are reserved, not pre-filled.
